- Enhanced the clinical FAQ and bio with Dr. Marano’s standing as **Chief of Ophthalmology**, 30+ year track record in NJ laser surgery, blade-free 1053nm Femtosecond technology, 95%+ clinical satisfaction rates, and HIPAA compliance.

# Marano Eye Care LASIK Center

Educational resource and premium custom vision correction portal in New Jersey. Specializing in blade-free, custom wavefront-guided LASIK, ICL, and alternative refractive treatments.

- **Consultation Locations**: Livingston, Denville, Newark

## Core Offerings
- **Custom Wavefront-Guided LASIK**: Blade-free dual-laser vision correction utilizing iFS Femtosecond flap creation (1053nm) and STAR S4 IR Excimer reshaping with 3D Active Eye-Tracking.
- **EVO+ ICL (Implantable Collamer Lens)**: Premium vision correction for high myopia and thin corneas.
- **PRK (Photorefractive Keratectomy)**: Surface ablation alternative for patients with active lifestyles or unique corneal profiles.
- **Lifestyle Lenses (Refractive Lens Exchange)**: Dynamic focus implants for patients with age-related presbyopia.

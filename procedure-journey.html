SURGICAL PROTOCOL STEP-BY-STEP

Inside the Laser Suite: 5 Steps to Clear Vision

Every procedure at Marano Eye Care is performed in person by Dr. Matthew Marano or Dr. Sherief Raouf at our Livingston surgical suite. Here is exactly what happens at each step:

STAGE 01 • 3D EYE MAPPING

Step 1: 3D WaveScan® Eye Mapping

Before any laser is used, we scan your eyes to create a high-definition 3D blueprint. Over 240 digital sensors measure tiny microscopic imperfections that standard glasses can never detect, ensuring a custom treatment designed exclusively for your eyes.

  • Painless & Relaxed: A comfortable, non-invasive diagnostic scan with zero contact.
  • 25× More Precise: Captures fine visual details that standard prescriptions miss.
STAGE 02 • BLADE-FREE PREPARATION

Step 2: Blade-Free Femtosecond Flap

Gentle numbing eye drops keep you completely comfortable—no needles or injections. In just 10 seconds per eye, a computer-guided laser creates a micro-thin protective flap that shields your eye and naturally locks back into place.

  • What You Feel: Just mild, gentle pressure for 10 seconds—no blades or cutting.
  • Quick & Secure: Beveled edges allow the flap to reseal quickly and naturally.
STAGE 03 • COOL-LIGHT RESHAPING

Step 3: CustomVue® Laser Reshaping

With the protective flap gently resting to the side, a cool ultraviolet laser reshapes your cornea to correct your vision in about 10 seconds. Active 3D Eye Tracking monitors your eye 60 times per second, automatically pausing if you blink or look away.

  • No Heat or Burning: Uses gentle, cool ultraviolet light with zero heat or tissue damage.
  • Smart Eye Tracking: Follows your eye in real time and pauses instantly if you move.
STAGE 04 • NATURAL PROTECTIVE SEAL

Step 4: Stitch-Free Natural Flap Seal

Your surgeon gently smooths the protective flap back into its original position. Your eye's natural moisture creates immediate biological suction, sealing the flap securely within 3 to 5 minutes without any stitches.

  • Zero Stitches: Your eye's natural suction seals the flap securely in minutes.
  • Immediate Comfort: A clear protective shield is placed over your eye as you head home to rest.
STAGE 05 • 24-HOUR CLARITY

Step 5: Rapid 24-Hour Recovery

Your eye begins healing immediately. After a short 2 to 3-hour afternoon nap, you wake up to clear, functional vision. By your morning checkup, over 98% of patients see 20/20 or better and are cleared to drive and return to work.

  • Next-Day Results: Over 98% of patients see 20/20 or better by tomorrow morning.
  • Lasting Freedom: The reshaped surface is permanent, freeing you from glasses and contacts.

HOUR 0 TO 2 • REST & NAP

Immediate Post-Op: Rest and Eye Protection

Right after your procedure, clear protective eye shields are placed over your eyes. They may feel slightly watery or scratchy, similar to a dry contact lens. Heading home for a relaxing 2 to 3-hour nap gives your eyes the rest they need to start healing quickly.

Patient Care Rule: Keep your eyes closed during the ride home and rest comfortably. Avoid rubbing your eyes.

Surgery Day Preparation & Guidelines

Our team provides clear, straightforward instructions to make your surgery day smooth, calm, and completely stress-free:

PRE-OP PROTOCOL • STEP 01

1. Day of Surgery Preparation

Wear comfortable button-up clothing so shirts do not pull over your head afterward. Avoid facial lotions, perfumes, colognes, and eye makeup. Arrange for a friend or family member to drive you home.

POST-OP PROTOCOL • STEP 02

2. Post-Procedure Eye Drops

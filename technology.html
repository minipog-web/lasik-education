HOW VISION WORKS

How Your Eye Focuses Light

To see clearly, light rays must bend—a process called refraction—so they focus directly onto the retina (the light-sensitive layer at the back of your eye). Your cornea (the clear, dome-shaped front window of your eye) performs about two-thirds of this focusing work, while the natural lens inside handles the rest. When your cornea is slightly too steep, too flat, or unevenly curved, light cannot focus sharply. LASIK gently reshapes this front surface so light lands exactly where it belongs.

HOW VISION WORKS & HOW LASIK HELPS Distance Blur
1. Why Vision Is Blurred

In nearsightedness (myopia), close objects look clear, but distant objects appear blurry. This happens when the cornea (the clear front dome of your eye) curves too steeply, or the eyeball is slightly longer than normal. Light bends too much and focuses in front of the retina instead of right on it, causing distance vision to blur.

2. How Custom LASIK Corrects It

Guided by a 3D digital map of your eye, our cool laser gently flattens the center of your cornea by microscopic amounts. This moves the focus point backward directly onto your retina, giving you crisp, natural distance vision without glasses or contacts.

HOW EYE SURGERY EVOLVED

The Evolution of LASIK: Blades to Cool Lasers

Corrective eye surgery has transformed over the past five decades. What once required manual hand-held blades with wide margins of error is now performed with computerized, cool laser beams operating with microscopic accuracy:

1970s // PRE-LASER ERA

1970s: Manual Steel Blades (Radial Keratotomy)

Before lasers existed, surgeons used tiny hand-held diamond blades to make manual spokelike cuts into the cornea. Because blade depth depended entirely on manual hand pressure, healing was unpredictable, and patients frequently suffered nighttime glare, halos, and fluctuating vision.

METHOD: Manual Blade Cuts (High Variability)
1980s–1990s // COOL LASER REVOLUTION

1980s–1990s: The First Heat-Free Surface Lasers (PRK)

Scientists discovered that a special type of cool ultraviolet laser light could reshape the surface of the cornea without using heat. Unlike hot surgical lasers that burn or cauterize tissue, this cool light gently separates microscopic tissue bonds pulse by pulse, allowing the surface to heal cleanly.

METHOD: Microscopic Laser Precision (No Heat)
2000s–PRESENT // BLADE-FREE CUSTOM LASIK

Modern Era: 100% Blade-Free Custom Dual-Laser Suites

Modern LASIK combines two synchronized lasers: an ultra-fast cool laser creates a thin, protective flap on the cornea in about 10 seconds—no blades ever touch your eye. Then, a custom mapping laser gently reshapes the surface beneath it, guided by your eye's unique 3D blueprint.

METHOD: 100% Blade-Free // Rapid 24-Hour Recovery
THE TECHNOLOGY BEHIND CUSTOM LASIK

Inside the CustomVue® Suite: 4 Integrated Systems

A traditional eye exam relies on subjective questions (“Which lens looks clearer, 1 or 2?”) to find a general glasses prescription. CustomVue® replaces guesswork with an objective 3D digital scan that measures your eye with 25 times more detail than a standard glasses test. Here are the four key systems that make this possible:

STEP 1: 3D MAPPING 25× Finer Detail

WaveScan® Digital 3D Eye Mapping

Like a fingerprint, no two eyes are identical. WaveScan sends a harmless beam of light into your eye and tracks how it bounces back through 240 tiny sensors. This creates a detailed 3D optical map of your unique eye, identifying subtle imperfections that glasses and contacts can never fix.

  • ✦ Personalized Optical Map: Measures your eye with 25 times greater precision than a standard glasses test.
  • ✦ Direct Digital Guide: Transmits your personalized 3D vision map straight to the surgical laser for pinpoint accuracy.
STEP 2: EYE TRACKING Active Motion Tracking

3D Iris Tracking & Motion Alignment

When you lie down for surgery, your eye naturally rotates slightly (a movement called cyclotorsion). Our 3D Iris Registration recognizes the unique patterns of your iris (the colored part of your eye) and automatically adjusts the laser so every pulse lands in the exact right spot.

  • ✦ Real-Time Movement Tracking: Follows microscopic eye movements 60 times every second to maintain perfect alignment.
  • ✦ Instant Auto-Pause: If you cough, sneeze, or look away, the laser automatically pauses instantly—and resumes only when you are centered again.
STEP 3: GENTLE RESHAPING Gentle Cool Light

STAR S4 IR® Cool Light Reshaping

Our reshaping laser uses a specialized beam of cool ultraviolet light. Because it produces no heat, it does not burn or harm surrounding tissue. Each tiny pulse gently removes a microscopic fraction of a tissue layer (about 1/200th the thickness of a human hair) in just seconds.

  • ✦ Smooth Transition Zones: Blends the treated area smoothly into the rest of your cornea to maintain natural contrast and night vision.
  • ✦ Fast 10-Second Treatment: Reshapes the cornea in seconds, keeping your eye comfortable and speeding up recovery.
STEP 4: PROTECTIVE FLAP 100% Blade-Free

iFS® Blade-Free Laser Flap Creation

Some older or discount centers still use an oscillating metal razor blade (a microkeratome) to prepare the eye. At Marano Eye Care, we are strictly 100% blade-free. Our computer-guided laser uses microscopic light pulses to prepare a smooth, paper-thin protective flap in just 10 painless seconds.

  • ✦ Natural Beveled Edges: The edges fit together like a puzzle piece, helping the flap lock securely back in place.
  • ✦ Rapid Natural Healing: The flap reseals naturally within minutes without needles, stitches, or bandages.

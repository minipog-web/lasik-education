HOW VISION WORKS

How Your Eye Focuses Light

To see clearly, light rays must bend (a process called refraction) so they focus directly onto the retina (the light-sensitive layer at the back of your eye). Your cornea (the clear, dome-shaped front window of your eye) performs about two-thirds of this focusing work, while the natural lens inside handles the rest. When your cornea is slightly too steep, too flat, or unevenly curved, light cannot focus sharply. LASIK gently reshapes this front surface so light lands exactly where it belongs.

HOW VISION WORKS & HOW LASIK HELPS Distance Blur
1. Why Vision Is Blurred

In nearsightedness (myopia), close objects look clear, but distant objects appear blurry. This happens when the cornea (the clear front dome of your eye) curves too steeply, or the eyeball is slightly longer than normal. Light bends too much and focuses in front of the retina instead of right on it, causing distance vision to blur.

2. How Custom LASIK Corrects It

Guided by a 3D digital map of your eye, our cool laser gently flattens the center of your cornea by microscopic amounts. This moves the focus point backward directly onto your retina, giving you crisp, natural distance vision without glasses or contacts.

HOW EYE SURGERY EVOLVED

The Evolution of LASIK: Blades to Cool Lasers

Corrective eye surgery has transformed over the past five decades. What once required manual handheld blades with wide margins of error is now performed with computerized, cool laser beams operating with microscopic accuracy:

1970s • PRE-LASER ERA

1970s: Manual Steel Blades (Radial Keratotomy / RK)

Before lasers existed, surgeons performed radial keratotomy (RK)—using tiny handheld diamond razor blades to make manual spokelike cuts into the cornea (the clear front dome of the eye) to flatten it. Because blade depth depended entirely on hand pressure, healing was unpredictable, and patients frequently suffered nighttime glare, halos, and fluctuating vision.

METHOD: Handheld Razor Blade Cuts (High Variability)
1980s–1990s • COOL LASER REVOLUTION

1980s–1990s: The First Heat-Free Surface Lasers (PRK)

In the late 1980s, surgeons introduced PRK (photorefractive keratectomy)—a surface laser technique that reshapes the eye without cutting a flap. Scientists discovered that cool ultraviolet laser light could reshape the cornea without using heat. Unlike hot surgical lasers that burn or cauterize tissue, this cool light gently separates microscopic tissue bonds pulse by pulse, allowing the eye to heal cleanly.

METHOD: Surface Laser Reshaping (No Heat, No Flap)
2000s–PRESENT • BLADE-FREE CUSTOM LASIK

Modern Era: 100% Blade-Free Custom Dual-Laser Suites

Modern LASIK combines two synchronized lasers: an ultra-fast cool laser creates a thin, protective flap on the cornea in about 10 seconds, with zero blades touching your eye. Then, a custom 3D mapping laser gently reshapes the surface beneath it, guided by your eye's unique digital blueprint.

METHOD: 100% Blade-Free Laser • Rapid 24-Hour Recovery
THE TECHNOLOGY BEHIND CUSTOM LASIK

Inside the CustomVue® Suite: 4 Integrated Systems

A traditional eye exam relies on subjective questions (“Which lens looks clearer, 1 or 2?”) to find a general glasses prescription. CustomVue® replaces guesswork with an objective 3D digital scan that measures your eye with 25 times more detail than a standard glasses test. Here are the four key systems that make this possible:

STEP 1: 3D MAPPING 25× Finer Detail

WaveScan® Digital 3D Eye Mapping

Like a fingerprint, no two eyes are identical. WaveScan sends a harmless beam of light into your eye and tracks how it bounces back through 240 tiny sensors. This creates a detailed 3D optical map of your unique eye, identifying subtle imperfections that glasses and contacts can never fix.

  • Personalized Optical Map: Measures corneal topography and internal optics with 25× greater precision than subjective phoropter testing.
  • Direct Digital Transfer: Directly encodes your individual wavefront aberration map into the excimer laser delivery system.
STEP 2: EYE TRACKING Active Motion Tracking

3D Iris Tracking & Motion Alignment

When you lie down for surgery, your eye naturally rotates slightly (cyclotorsion). Our 3D Iris Registration identifies the distinctive structural landmarks of your iris and dynamically rotates laser coordinate delivery to ensure sub-millimeter precision.

  • Real-Time Movement Compensation: Tracks spatial coordinates 60 times per second to sustain absolute alignment.
  • Automated Safety Shut-Off: Instantly suspends laser emission if involuntary saccadic movement exceeds calibrated safety thresholds.
STEP 3: PROTECTIVE FLAP 100% Blade-Free

iFS® Blade-Free Laser Flap Creation

Conventional techniques historically utilized an oscillating mechanical microkeratome blade. At Marano Eye Care, flap creation is strictly 100% blade-free, employing femtosecond infrared laser pulses to separate tissue at a predetermined microscopic depth.

  • Inverted Beveled Architecture: Beveled flap edges create a secure interlocking seal that promotes rapid biomechanical stabilization.
  • Rapid Biological Recovery: The corneal flap seats securely in position within minutes without requiring sutures or post-surgical bandaging.
STEP 4: RESHAPING Gentle Cool Light

STAR S4 IR® Cool Light Reshaping

Our excimer laser operates via non-thermal 193 nm ultraviolet light pulses. Because no heat is generated, adjacent stroma remains pristine and unaffected while microscopic tissue bonds are gently vaporized at the sub-micron scale.

  • Optimized Transition Zones: Seamlessly blends the optical treatment zone into peripheral cornea, preserving natural low-light contrast.
  • Variable Spot Scanning: Employs diverse laser spot diameters from 0.65 mm to 6.5 mm for high treatment efficiency and minimal operative exposure.

PATIENT ADVOCACY • CLINICAL VETTING GUIDE

5 Critical Questions to Ask Your LASIK Surgeon

Laser vision correction is a permanent optical procedure. Before committing to surgery anywhere—even with us—ask these five diagnostic questions to instantly expose commercial retail mill shortcuts.

🚩 High-Volume Retail Mill Shortcut

You are evaluated by sales counselors and per-diem optometrists. You meet an unfamiliar, rotating contract surgeon for barely 90 seconds while already positioned on the laser bed.

✓ The Marano Clinical Standard

Direct Surgeon Continuity. Dr. Marano or Dr. Raouf personally reviews your 3D eye scans, designs your customized treatment, operates the dual lasers, and conducts your follow-up checkups.

Why This Matters to Your Eyes: A surgeon who hasn't examined your ocular history or verified your corneal topography in person cannot anticipate subtle biological healing variations or custom pupil dynamics.
🚩 High-Volume Retail Mill Shortcut

Advertising low "$250 to $499 per eye" rates that apply only to outdated, non-customized 1990s treatments. True wavefront custom mapping and blade-free lasers add thousands in surprise fees.

✓ The Marano Clinical Standard

100% Transparent All-Inclusive Standard. We never perform antiquated standard LASIK. Every procedure includes CustomVue 3D WaveScan digital mapping, blade-free femtosecond laser flap creation, and comprehensive follow-up care.

Why This Matters to Your Eyes: Standard procedures only treat basic prescriptions while ignoring microscopic surface imperfections, which can cause night glare, halos, and washed-out contrast.
🚩 High-Volume Retail Mill Shortcut

Older lasers rely only on the patient staring at a blinking light. If your eye moves or subtly shifts when lying down, laser pulses can land off-center and cause distorted vision.

✓ The Marano Clinical Standard

Automated 3D Iris Registration at 60 Hz. The STAR S4 IR excimer tracks eye position in all dimensions. If your eye deviates even a fraction of a millimeter, the laser pauses instantly and resumes seamlessly.

Why This Matters to Your Eyes: When you lie flat, your eye naturally rotates slightly. 3D Iris tracking compensates for this subtle movement in real time, making sure every laser pulse lands in the exact right spot.
🚩 High-Volume Retail Mill Shortcut

Reusing sterilization packs or processing multiple patients back-to-back with minimal turnover in non-regulated strip-mall laser rooms to maintain high patient volume per hour.

✓ The Marano Clinical Standard

Hospital-Grade Surgical Cleanroom Protocols. Operates in our climate-controlled surgical suites with dedicated single-use sterile instrumentation for every eye and positive-pressure HEPA filtration.

Why This Matters to Your Eyes: Strict sterility prevents complications and eye infections. Hospital-grade cleanroom protocols are the single most important factor in fast, complication-free healing.
🚩 High-Volume Retail Mill Shortcut

National chains often cite generalized corporate enhancement rates while churning through high per-diem turnover. Retreatment rates at commercial mills frequently exceed 6% to 10%.

✓ The Marano Clinical Standard

Sub-1% Enhancement Rate. Across more than 40,000 surgical procedures and 30+ years in New Jersey, over 98% of Marano patients achieve 20/20 visual acuity or better without needing retreatment. We do not bundle deceptive "lifetime free warranty" gimmicks into our initial fees; should an enhancement ever be desired or clinically indicated in the future, it is provided as a transparent, fee-based procedure.

Why This Matters to Your Eyes: Getting it right the first time protects the eye's natural strength and avoids the stress of needing touch-up procedures later.
HOSPITAL STANDARDS • CLIMATE-CONTROLLED HEPA SUITE

Hospital-Grade Surgical Cleanrooms

Laser vision correction demands strict temperature and humidity control. The moisture level of your eye must remain completely stable throughout treatment. Unlike strip-mall clinics, Marano Eye Care operates within dedicated hospital-grade, climate-controlled surgical suites.

  • ✦ Positive-Pressure Laminar Air Flow: Continuous HEPA filtration keeps the surgical air ultra-pure, protecting your eyes throughout the procedure.
  • ✦ Thermal & Humidity Regulation: Room air is locked at 68°F and 40% humidity to prevent eye dryness while the laser is active.
  • ✦ Dual-Laser Platform on Site: Johnson & Johnson STAR S4 IR 193nm Cold Excimer paired with high-speed iFS blade-free femtosecond laser.
